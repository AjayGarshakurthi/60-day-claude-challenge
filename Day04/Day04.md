## 4. Learnings
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting helps AI generate more structured and detailed responses by breaking a problem into smaller steps.
Instead of providing a direct answer, CoT prompting encourages logical reasoning and step-by-step thinking.
It improves the quality and accuracy of responses, especially for planning and decision-making tasks.
CoT prompting can be used for career planning, problem-solving, project development, and learning new concepts. 
## How roadmap was generated?
The AI first collected information about my current skills, interests, career goals, and available learning time.
It analyzed the information and identified the skills required to achieve my target career path.
The roadmap was then divided into short-term, medium-term, and long-term goals.
Each stage included specific learning objectives, technologies to focus on, and practical activities such as projects and skill development.
The final roadmap provided a clear learning path and actionable steps to help me progress toward my career goals.
## 5. Key Insight
 My biggest takeaways are,
 I don't need to learn everything; AI/ML and cloud skills are the highest-priority gaps.
Building real AI projects is more valuable than studying theory alone.
The fastest route to an AI role is: ML basics → LLM applications → deployed portfolio projects → job applications/networking.

Providing detailed and accurate answers resulted in a more personalized roadmap.
